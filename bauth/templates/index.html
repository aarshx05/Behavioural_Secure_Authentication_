These attributes are recorded with every sample and scored as a second layer alongside your typing rhythm. They are deliberately kept out of the machine learning feature vector — during enrollment they never change, so a classifier would simply learn “this IP means authentic”, which is trivially spoofed and breaks the moment you switch network.

Keystrokes are timed in your browser and never leave it as raw text beyond the password you are authenticating with. Profiles are stored locally on the server.
